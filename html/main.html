About Situflex

Situflex was built in Nairobi to make it easier to find and reach the beautiful places in our own region. Many of the best spots are hard to find online, so we are bringing them together in one place, with clear locations and honest travel tips.

Our colours carry meaning: green for nature, white for peace, black for the people, and red for blood.
