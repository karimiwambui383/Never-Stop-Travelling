How Tu-Flex works

Tu-Flex helps you find beautiful places across Kenya and East Africa, see where they are, and decide where to go next.

Find your next trip in three steps

  1. Create your account

    Register with your email, then log in. Your account is where your saved places will live.

    Log in or register

  2. Discover places

    Browse places grouped by what you want to do: wildlife, hikes, coast and culture. Each place shows its county and a short description.

    Start discovering

  3. See it on the map

    Open any place on the map to see exactly where it is. Use the map to spot nearby places and group them into one trip.

    Open the map

What you can explore

  • Wildlife

    National parks and reserves, from the Maasai Mara to Amboseli.

  • Hikes

    Mountains, gorges and trails for every fitness level.

  • Coast

    Beaches and ocean towns along the Indian Ocean.

  • Culture

    Historic towns, forts and living traditions.

Common questions

Do I need an account to browse?
You can browse places and the map without one. An account is for saving places.
Is Tuflex only for Kenya?
We start with Kenya and are growing across East Africa.
Is it free?
Yes. Discovering places and using the map costs nothing.
